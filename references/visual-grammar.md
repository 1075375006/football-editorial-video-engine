# Visual Grammar v0.1

FEE is editorial collage that moves. Use a cream/paper base, muted texture, strong asymmetry, heavy or condensed sans type, neutral ink, and one or two high-saturation accents. A screen carries one main claim. Reveal quickly in roughly 10–18 frames at 30fps and hold long enough to read.

## Must keep

- Paper, field, city, or map environments are quiet enough for information.
- Cutout or illustration subjects have a clear silhouette and negative space.
- Arrows, circles, labels, and pitch geometry explain the mechanism.
- Graphic wipes and paper blocks are preferred to generic dissolves.
- Information and annotations remain stable during a parallax drift.

## Avoid

Glass panels, HUDs, gratuitous glow, perpetual floating, full-scene gradients, a shadow on every element, universal spring physics, and text or data drawn inside generated images. FEE is moving graphic design, not a template full of effects or a dashboard.

## Palette

```text
paper   #F1E9D8
ink     #1F1E1B
muted   #827B6E
teamA   #E4572E
teamB   #2F5D8C
accent  #E6B84A
warning #D6902F
out     #B9473D
```

The engine exposes these as tokens. A production project may replace team colors per match, but should retain the paper/ink relationship and cap high-saturation accents at one or two.
